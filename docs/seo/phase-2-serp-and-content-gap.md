# Phase 2 SERP and content-gap review

Review date: 2026-07-30. Market: Romanian-language organic results. This is qualitative intent research; no search-volume estimates are claimed.

## Observed demand and result patterns

Results consistently group around calculators and sizing, the number of panels, 5 kW packages and production, payback, battery comparisons, and offer verification. Many ranking commercial pages collapse distinct questions into a product pitch or publish a single production/payback number without exposing locality, autoconsumption, loss, tariff, or cost assumptions.

RaportSolar addresses that gap with six focused pages. Each page identifies inputs, uncertainty, and a next step in an existing tool. The pages do not claim guaranteed output, savings, or payback.

## Ownership and cannibalization

- Calculator intent belongs to `/calculator-panouri-fotovoltaice`; technical engineering depth remains at `/dimensionare-sistem-fotovoltaic`.
- Module-count intent belongs to `/cate-panouri-fotovoltaice-imi-trebuie`.
- Payback methodology belongs to `/amortizare-sistem-fotovoltaic`; autoconsumption definitions remain at `/autoconsum-energie-solara`.
- Combined production/economy intent belongs to `/productie-si-economii-panouri-fotovoltaice`; production fundamentals remain at `/productie-panouri-fotovoltaice`.
- Decision comparison belongs to `/sistem-fotovoltaic-cu-baterie-sau-fara`; battery specifications remain at `/baterie-pentru-panouri-fotovoltaice`.
- The existing `/cum-verifici-o-oferta-fotovoltaica` remains the offer-comparison owner. No duplicate page was created.

## Deliberate exclusions

Casa Verde/AFM content is deferred because the official program page located during research clearly described the 2024 program, while a current 2026 application state was not established from a primary source. County and city landing-page expansion is rejected: it would create thin doorway patterns without unique, supportable local evidence.
